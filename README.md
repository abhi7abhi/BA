## 📌 Repository Overview

This repository houses hands-on **Business Analytics (BA)** coursework, interactive **Microsoft Power BI** reports (`.pbix`), executive presentations, and quantitative business case studies.

---

## 📑 Portfolio Contents & Reports

1. **Assignment 1 & 2 (`Assignment-1(PBI).pbix`, `Assignment-2.pbix`)**:
   - Customer segmentation, sales performance by region, and time-series trend forecasting.
   - Customized DAX calculated columns and dynamic KPI measures.
2. **Assignment 4 & 6 (`Assignment-4.pbix`, `Assign-4.pdf`, `Assignment6`)**:
   - Multi-dimensional data modeling, star schema relationships, and interactive slice-and-dice visualizations.
3. **Executive Documentation (`ABHI.pdf`, `Assignment-2.pdf`)**:
   - Comprehensive analytical interpretations, business recommendations, and strategy deliverables.
